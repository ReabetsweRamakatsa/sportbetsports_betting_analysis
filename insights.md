# Key Insights & Business Recommendations

# Key Insights

# 1️ KYC Verification Is High but Still a Revenue Lever

A strong majority of users have completed KYC verification.

KYC-verified users show significantly higher average deposits than non-verified users.

This confirms that identity verification correlates with higher trust and spending.

  # Business implication:
KYC is not just a compliance requirement — it directly impacts revenue quality.

# 2️ Betting Revenue Is Concentrated in a Few Sports

Football (Soccer) generates the highest Gross Gaming Revenue (GGR).

Rugby and Cricket follow, while other sports contribute marginal revenue.

Revenue distribution reflects South African sports consumption patterns.

  #Business implication:
A small number of sports drive the majority of platform profitability.

# 3️ Certain Bet Types Are Disproportionately Profitable

Simpler bet types (e.g. Match Result, 1X2 markets) show higher profitability.

High-odds or complex bet types generate volume but also higher payouts.

Some bet types act more as engagement tools than profit drivers.

  # Business implication:
Not all bets should be evaluated equally — some exist to retain customers rather than maximize margin.

# 4️ Customer Value Is Highly Skewed

A small segment of users accounts for a large share of total betting volume.

The top bettors demonstrate significantly higher lifetime betting value.

This aligns with typical Pareto (80/20) behavior in betting platforms.

  # Business implication:
Customer lifetime value (CLV) varies dramatically — mass-market strategies alone are inefficient.

# 5️ Positive Gross Gaming Revenue Indicates Sustainable Margin

Total stakes exceed total payouts, resulting in positive GGR.

Margin varies by sport and bet type, indicating optimization opportunities.

The platform shows healthy revenue fundamentals.

  # Business implication:
The business model is viable, but margin optimization is possible with better product steering.

# Strategic Recommendations
  # 1️ Accelerate KYC Completion Through Incentives

Offer small free bets or deposit bonuses upon KYC completion.

Use in-app nudges for high-activity but non-KYC users.

Prioritize early KYC completion during onboarding.

# Expected impact: Higher deposits, lower fraud risk, stronger compliance posture.

  # 2️ Double Down on High-Performing Sports

Allocate marketing spend primarily to football, rugby, and cricket.

Create league-specific promotions for top-performing competitions.

De-prioritize low-margin sports or reposition them for engagement only.

# Expected impact: Improved ROI on marketing and higher GGR efficiency.

  # 3️ Actively Manage Bet Type Mix

Promote high-margin bet types in default bet slips.

Limit exposure to consistently low-margin or volatile bet types.

Use complex bets as retention tools, not revenue anchors.

# Expected impact: Improved profitability without reducing user engagement.

  # 4️ Segment and Protect High-Value Customers

Identify top bettors using lifetime stake or net revenue.

Offer personalized odds boosts, VIP support, or loyalty rewards.

Monitor for responsible gambling thresholds.

# Expected impact: Higher retention and controlled risk on high-value accounts.

  # 5️ Use Data to Support Responsible Gambling

Monitor unusually high betting frequency or stake growth.

Introduce soft limits or cooling-off prompts.

Align analytics with regulatory and ethical standards.

📈 Expected impact: Reduced regulatory risk and long-term platform sustainability.
